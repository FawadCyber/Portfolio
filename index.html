About

Curiosity is good.
Shipping is better.

I’m Fawad, a self-taught automation engineer who likes the work hiding beneath polished interfaces: authentication flows, awkward payloads, undocumented limits, and systems that need to stay useful after the demo.

My recent focus is reusable AI infrastructure, especially MCP servers that give assistants safe, practical access to external services, and the production apps that sit on top of it: a PostgreSQL-backed helpdesk, webhook-served dashboards, and desktop builds with Tauri.
